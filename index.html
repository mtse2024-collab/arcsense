    Why T12 and S2?

    Mjøsund et al. (2017) placed two motion sensors at T12 and S2 on 34 people, 18 of them with low back pain, and compared them with lab motion capture. Average error was about 1.8° for forward bending and under 1° for backward and side bending. Rotation was not tested, and the study used single-plane movements at a comfortable speed, so twisting and fast sport movements still need their own validation. ArcSense will report its own error against motion capture or a digital inclinometer.
